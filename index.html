The History of Halloween

Halloween is a holiday celebrated every year on October 31st. Its history can be traced back to ancient traditions and festivals that marked the changing of the seasons.

One of the traditions connected to Halloween is the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain. People believed that during this time the boundary between the living and the dead became less clear. Over time, different traditions and customs combined to create the Halloween celebration we know today.

Today, Halloween is celebrated with costumes, decorations, pumpkins, trick-or-treating, parties, and other spooky activities.
